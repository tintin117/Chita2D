# Colosseum art

The sandstone floor atlas was drawn for the game's 2:1 ground grid. The arcade and its shadows are drawn from the same rim as the wall collider. The brazier and broken masonry sprites were made with built-in ImageGen and sized to the original heroes.

`canyon.png` is an original ImageGen background plate with sandstone cliffs, a sandy approach, and small camps near the edges. It was generated from the supplied composition reference with an empty central clearing for the editable Godot bowl.

## Sprite prompt

Create an original transparent sprite atlas for a playable ruined red sandstone colosseum, fixed 2:1 orthographic isometric ground, matching an authentic low-resolution pixelated 3D fantasy arena style. The attached sketch is reference only for ancient elevated arena motifs and lighting scale. Four separate objects in strict 2x2 panels, generous clear alpha around and between them. Top left: massive Roman-style rust-red sandstone gate arch made from thick chipped ochre blocks, dark recessed bronze portcullis, cracked short pillars on both sides; front to the viewer, the base runs left to right with a shallow isometric depth. Top right: a matching rough red sandstone pedestal topped by a bronze bowl with small intense amber and yellow flames, subtle soot, floor-level base. Bottom left: two narrow tattered crimson and burnt-orange standards on one low crumbling ochre plinth, cloth hangs downward with torn tips, a simple woven gold stripe. Bottom right: toppled red sandstone column alongside a low broken wall and large broken blocks, a compact readable piece of combat cover. All assets fully visible. Ground contact is clearly defined, short or no baked shadows, no backdrop, no floor tile, no perspective convergence, no text or watermark. Use natural red ochre, burnt orange, warm gold, and umber. Preserve material grain and textured pixel clusters and avoid hard ink outlines.

## Side gate sprites

The west and east gates use the top half of a second built-in ImageGen atlas. The prompt for those sprites follows.

Create an original transparent 2x2 game sprite sheet, each quadrant isolated with broad clear gutters. Use attached sandstone sprite atlas only as style/material/scale reference. Isometric orthographic 2:1 pixelated fantasy game, medieval Roman ruins, fire-colored weathered stone. TOP LEFT: an isometric rust-red sandstone portcullis doorway for the rear-left curved arena wall, with front face turned toward camera right and viewer; its ground baseline goes UP toward the right, exactly slope -1/2 in image space. Show clear diagonal direction via sandstone cap, front face and side thickness, upright vertical columns and dark bronze iron grate. TOP RIGHT: counterpart for rear-right facing camera left, ground baseline goes DOWN toward right exactly slope +1/2, use same upper-left lighting. These are compact flanking doors, below full arch facade height, weathered, no platform.
