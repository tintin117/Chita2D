# Brookside Village art

The inn, bakery, stall, well, planter, tree, barrel grouping, and garden wall were generated with built-in ImageGen. The cobble atlas was authored for the game's 2:1 ground grid. The generated images were cropped and resized with their alpha channels intact. Each placed prop has a ground contact point and a soft shadow sized to its footprint.

## Building and market props prompt

Create an original game-production sprite atlas in a 2 by 2 layout, output landscape or square with four clearly isolated sprites in strict quadrants separated by large fully transparent gutters. The attached medieval village image is reference ONLY for muted material palette, isometric camera and pixelated shaded surfaces. Design new original forms. Fixed orthographic isometric view for all four, ground axes 2 horizontal to 1 vertical, vertical walls vertical. Directional light from upper left, darker right side. Low-resolution 3D-rendered pixel art with natural chunky pixel clusters and irregular stone texture, earthy medieval game, no outlines and no painted backdrop. Top-left: compact two-story timber and rough stone house with an oxblood clay tile gable roof, small chimney, carved oak door with an amber lantern, shuttered windows and a mossy foundation. Top-right: smaller single-story stone bakery with an ochre shingle roof, chimney and blue shutter, squat slightly asymmetric footprint. Bottom-left: small low burgundy canvas market stall over wooden counter with a few goods and timber lattice, completely freestanding. Bottom-right: small octagonal stepped stone well with clear desaturated teal water, no canopy. Each silhouette fully visible with ground contact base, at most a tiny soft contact shadow, no ground tile and no extended cast shadow. Each object must have ample transparent margin around all edges, no overlap, no lettering, no characters. Shape and architectural height comparable to the game reference.

The supplied village screenshot was a style and camera reference.
