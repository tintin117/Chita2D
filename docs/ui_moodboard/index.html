Chita sprites and terrain

The original idle silhouettes and terrain are the source for the roster, menu composition and UI colors.

Rules for Chita

  1. Compose the backdrop and portraits from the game’s tiles and idle frames on a whole-pixel grid.
  2. Use flat fills, one native-pixel seams and fixed 9-slice corners. Reserve bright grass for selection and focus.
  3. Use simple pixel glyphs for skills and a chunky title silhouette. Give the type room to breathe.
  4. Prioritize HP, dash, cooldowns, match time and standings in PvP. Keep progression in PvE.
  5. Group choices by task. Keep one primary action clear and keep keyboard focus visible.
